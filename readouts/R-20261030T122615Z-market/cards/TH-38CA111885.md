## 03 WHY COULD STOCKS BENEFIT OR SUFFER?
- _Industry shift_ **SOURCE OPINION:** Open models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) reach frontier-closed capability faster each era per composite benchmarks. [C-509eb5ab8af711, C-e851266a6a6cba, C-acbdb749b632fa, C-98f3cd537c7d24]
- _Pricing_ **SOURCE OPINION:** Capable open models at a fraction of the cost could commoditize the model layer and compress what frontier labs can charge. [C-7329afdb8f8b53]
- _Margins_ **SOURCE OPINION:** Commoditization would be disastrous for frontier-lab margins, though the article does not quantify the effect and argues productization (e.g. Claude Code) preserves advantage. [C-7329afdb8f8b53, C-f7d37ff4ecf399]
- _Listed-company earnings_ **MODEL INFERENCE:** Downward price pressure would erode frontier labs' pricing power and revenue; the article quantifies no earnings effect.
