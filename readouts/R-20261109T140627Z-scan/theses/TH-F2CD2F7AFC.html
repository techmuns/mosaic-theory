The thesis

Amazon has made a major course correction toward a first-class native PyTorch (TorchNeuron) experience on Trainium3, which the publisher's hands-on testing found promising (23.89%/30.47% MFU on Qwen3-32B, 93% FlexAttention pass rate, SOTA profiler), though still far from CUDA maturity. source's view under review
