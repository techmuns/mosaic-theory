What could prove it wrong

  • The AI investment boom has had a much larger impact on earnings and market value than GDP; as the impulse peaks, markets may worry whether the record share of GDP going to US earnings can persist, risking a bigger aggregate shock and index-level drawdowns. (scope: US equities, especially AI-infrastructure-levered names; risk framed as higher for markets than the economy) source's view
  • Equity index volatility has risen a little and is unlikely to fall, with scope to rise further if macro stresses broaden, making longer-dated options and OTM puts more valuable as protection. (scope: major equity indices; sharpest volatility so far under the surface) source's view
  • GS expects further widening in US credit spreads with US high yield to underperform, warning that problems in the tail of weaker borrowers could create more turbulent conditions for broader financing; it draws a parallel to the sustained 1998-2000 divergence between credit and equity markets which may be more challenging now without the capital inflows and rate cuts of the 1997-98 EM crises. (scope: US credit / high-yield spreads in 2027; base case widening is modest but risks clearly skewed wider, acute if AI-boom profitability doubts grow or economic conditions deteriorate.) forecast under review
  • 3 more risks
    • GS forecasts US high-yield credit spreads to widen materially, from 320bp currently to 410bp in 12 months (90bp wider), with risks clearly skewed toward further widening. (scope: US HY credit spreads, 12-month baseline forecast with risk skew to wider spreads) forecast
    • We think the risk to markets is higher than the risk to the economic picture, because the boom itself has had its largest impact there. source's view
    • AI-related drawdowns could tighten financial conditions meaningfully, creating a feedback loop that turns a rotation into a bigger aggregate shock to markets and the economy. (scope: GS sees the risk to markets higher than to the economy; main channel to economic downside is through financial conditions. Conditional on transition failing.) source's view under review
