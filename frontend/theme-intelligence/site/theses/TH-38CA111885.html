What could prove it wrong

  • Although Kimi K scores higher than Fable 5 on the composite, SemiAnalysis still prefers using Fable for day-to-day work, partly due to Anthropic's productization. source's view
  • The authors argue they are less bearish on frontier models than the catch-up trend might initially suggest. source's view
  • While OpenAI and Google competed on leaderboards, Anthropic turned Claude into the default coding agent, setting the terms for the agentic era in which benchmarks are run in a terminal. source's view
  • 9 more risks
    • GPT-5.2 scored better on the benchmark suite than Opus 4.5 but did not deliver a better user experience; the full agentic product (model + harness) was what mattered. source's view
    • Benchmarks are an imperfect proxy for real work, especially public benchmarks that model makers can hill-climb by creating RL environments mimicking the tasks. source's view
    • China's safety regime is stricter than any in the world on content/process for public-facing services but looser than the EU and California on the frontier. source's view under review
    • Beijing's stated position is the most safety-forward of any major AI power outside Europe. source's view

    • Moonshot's Yang Zhilin, despite acknowledging AGI/ASI risks, chose acceleration, saying development should not be given up. source's view under review

Signals to watch (6)
  • Frontier labs sustaining or raising prices and ARR growth despite open-model gains
  • Open-model usage share stalling relative to closed APIs
  • Benchmark catch-up time widening again in the next era
  • China adopts binding frontier-capability duties (compute thresholds, pre-release safety cases)
  • Chinese labs begin routinely publishing dangerous-capability evaluations at launch
  • Chinese open-weight release cadence or open-weight share declines materially
Competing explanation
  • Benchmarks are not a perfect proxy for real work; SemiAnalysis still prefers Fable (closed) for daily work due to productization, and Era 3 catch-up may be artificially deflated by safety-testing delays. source's view
  • Beijing's stated position and Framework 3.0 are the most explicit official Chinese text on frontier risk, which could be read as an emerging safety brake rather than pure speed. source's view
