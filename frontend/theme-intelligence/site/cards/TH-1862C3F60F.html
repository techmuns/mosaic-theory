What changed

  • SpaceX and Qualcomm announced a multi-year supply agreement for SpaceX to adopt Qualcomm's Dragonwing 5G base station chipset solutions.
  • SpaceX secured access to approximately 80,000 base station sites via leasing arrangements with independent tower operators.
Details

Effect on profits, margins or demand

  • Industry shiftSpaceX adopts open-RAN Qualcomm chipsets instead of proprietary Ericsson/Nokia RAN for US mobile buildout.
  • VolumesRollout across ~80,000 leased sites drives chipset, antenna, power-amplifier and tower-leasing volumes for ecosystem suppliers.
  • CostsPre-integrated O-RAN chipsets substantially reduce SpaceX's cost of deploying tens of thousands of base stations.
