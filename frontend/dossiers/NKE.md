## The argument

**Evidence balance:** mixed — fortress balance sheet and a real Running/wholesale mechanism offset by still-falling forward estimates, a shrinking half of revenue, and a forward multiple above peers.

### Bull case — the decline is overdone
- Management points to a concrete demand mechanism: NIKE Running delivered 5 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth and added roughly $1B to the Running business, evidence the Sport Offense is producing where deployed. [10]
- Wholesale revenue grew 4% for the fiscal year led by double-digit North America growth, a channel-recovery driver behind the flat-but-stabilising top line. [10]
- Absolute EBITDA actually ROSE year-over-year from $4,477M (FY25) to $4,929M (FY26) even as the margin % sat near a decade-trough, the reconciler most consensus reads miss. [11]
- Roughly 5 points of the reported Q3 weakness was a deliberate, self-inflicted classic-footwear inventory cleanup management frames as improving marketplace health, arguing the trough is engineered rather than demand-driven. [30]
- Balance sheet is fortress-grade: net-debt/EBITDA 0.4x with interest coverage of 21.6x, self-funding and never near a survival question. [8]
- The name carries an S&P issuer rating of A+ and scores stronger than the peer median on all four credit-health legs (2/2/2/2 vs peer 3/3/3/3), so the de-rate is not a solvency story. [31]
- On its own 9.2-year history EV/EBITDA sits at 9.1x against a median 21.1x and a low of 11.2x — a 0th-percentile trough that POSES an overshoot, though the trailing-only basis leaves overshoot-vs-terminal unresolved. [32]

### Bear case — the decline is real
- The half of revenue that matters most is still shrinking: management expects Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear — ~half of revenue — to remain negative this fiscal year, with over $4B of classic-footwear revenue intentionally removed from peak. [10]
- Fundamentals underwriting the peer gap are genuinely worse where it counts: revenue growth 0% vs peer-median 7%, and the discount must be judged EARNED vs RESIDUAL against this. [12]
- Analyst breadth is one-sided down: FY27 EPS revisions 0 up / 3 down last month and near-term revenue revisions overwhelmingly negative (Revenue FQ1 3m 1↑/17↓), with the consensus rating cut to Hold and targets falling. [13]
- Profitability has structurally stepped down: ROCE fell to 10.4% (FY26) and 9.1% (FY25) from 16-22% in prior years, and margin % sits at a decade-low 11% EBITDA. [33]
- On a FORWARD basis the name is EXPENSIVE, not cheap: NTM TEV/EBITDA is 14.64x vs peer median 8.37x and NTM P/E 21.24x vs peer median 12.25x — the reverse of the trailing 'cheap vs peers' read once the cut base is priced in. [34]
- Forward estimates are collapsing, not stabilising — FY27 EBITDA consensus fell ~25% (from $5,056M to $3,796M over 12m) and FY27 normalized EPS fell ~31%, so the trailing 9.1x is on a base still being cut. [35]
- A named channel loss is dated and concrete: Nike notified Pou Sheng that its existing online sales of Nike products in mainland China terminate completely from January 1, 2027. [36]
- A discrete gap-down on 2026-04-01 (FQ3 results, 8-K item 2.02) marks the de-rate as event-driven off a specific earnings print, not a slow drift. [4]
- The de-rate is idiosyncratic, not market-driven: NKE returned -35.7% over 180d while SPY returned +10.1%, a -45.9% relative gap that isolates the problem to the name. [7]

### Decisive question
**Is the FY26 EBITDA trough the bottom of an engineered, self-inflicted inventory/brand reset that re-rates as the Sport Offense scales, or the early leg of a structural demand and margin impairment as classic-franchise revenue keeps fading?**

### …evidence for
- NIKE Running has now run 5 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth, adding ~$1B, and performance business grew mid-single digits — the mechanism management says will scale to other sports. [10]
- Roughly 5 points of the Q3 reported hit was deliberate classic-footwear inventory removal management frames as necessary marketplace-health cleanup, implying a temporary drag. [30]
- Absolute EBITDA rose FY25→FY26 ($4,477M→$4,929M) even at a decade-low margin %, consistent with a base beginning to inflect up. [11]

### …evidence against
- Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear — approximately half of revenue — are expected to stay negative this fiscal year, with improvement only in the back half. [10]
- FY27 consensus EBITDA has been cut ~25% over 12 months (to $3,796M) — below the FY26 $4,929M actual — so the forward path is still being marked down, not stabilised. [35]
- Revenue growth is 0% vs a peer median of 7%, and near-term revenue revision breadth is heavily negative (FQ1 3m 1↑/17↓). [12]

### Key unknowns
- Adjusted/forward EV/EBITDA and adjusted EPS are UNKNOWN — not in the revised guidance — so the trailing 9.1x cannot be separated cleanly into temporarily-depressed vs permanently-impaired; the overshoot-vs-terminal question stays open on the multiple alone.
- No segment EBITDA disclosure — revenue is segmented across 7 units but EBITDA is not — an expected disclosure gap that hides where the profit drag actually sits (bearish opacity).
- The magnitude and timing of the Sportswear/Jordan Streetwear inflection is unquantified; management gives only 'negative this year, back-half improvement' with no sized recovery.
- Greater China trajectory unresolved — an existing online channel (Pou Sheng) terminates Jan 1 2027 and management only cites 'high single digit' lift in must-win doors, leaving the net China direction unclear.
- CFO fell FY25→FY26 ($3,698M→$2,868M) and CIQ 'Levered FCF' ($1,889M) diverges 0.87x from CFO−capex ($2,184M) — which FCF basis underwrites the self-funding read is not settled.
- Peer set is flagged HETEROGENEOUS (caps $2.0B–$28.6B vs the name's $53.6B), so every peer multiple is a confidence-gated input, not a clean benchmark, and the earned-vs-residual discount cannot be read off the median.
- A new CFO (David Denton) took over Aug 16 2026 alongside multiple senior/board changes — execution continuity through the turnaround is unproven.
- The unverified export date (a FLOOR of ≥2026-09-20) means the revision path may be even more stale/negative than shown.
