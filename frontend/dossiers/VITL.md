## The case
_(top points; the full argument is in Analysis)_

_Bull case — the decline is overdone:_
- Management shows a direct, tested pricing lever: narrowing the gap at a top-10 customer from ~35% to ~25% above competing premium eggs lifted volumes 18% in two weeks versus the prior four — evidence the velocity problem is price-gap driven and reversible. [11]
- Distribution momentum is building independent of price: an at-least-50% TDP increase secured with a top-3 customer, direct distribution with a top-10 customer, category-captain role at another top-3 banner, and a guided 15–25% TDP add over 2026 — the single best yearly TDP gain since IPO. [11]

_Bear case — the decline is real:_
- The company is burning cash: LTM levered FCF -$53.9M (and CFO $9.9M − capex $99.6M = -$89.7M), FCF-negative on both bases, with survival capacity flagged as burning cash and the cash balance not derived — a genuine, load-bearing concern despite low debt. [12]

### Decisive question
