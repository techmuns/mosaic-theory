### Decisive question
**Is HLF's deep discount an OVERSHOOT on a business that has genuinely stabilised and returned to growth, or an EARNED value-trap discount on a levered, negative-equity direct-selling model in secular decline?**

### …evidence for
- Four consecutive quarters of YoY net-sales growth (Q2 2026 +5.4% reported, +5.8% constant currency), with India setting records — the top line that drove the de-rating has reversed. [10]
- Absolute EBITDA has climbed off the 2023 trough ($524M → $625M LTM) and the balance sheet was de-levered via the April refinancing, cutting ~$45M annual cash interest. [12]
- The name grows faster than the peer median with lower leverage (rev growth 6% vs 2%, Debt/EBITDA 3.2 vs 3.5) — leaving open a RESIDUAL, unexplained discount. [11]

### …evidence against
- FY2026 GAAP EPS consensus has been cut -33% over 12 months to 1.54 with recent breadth 0↑/2↓ — the analyst community is marking the business DOWN through the growth. [35]
- Equity is negative (-$474M; book value -$4.53/share) and credit health scores weaker than peers (Solvency/Liquidity 3/4 vs 2.5/2.5), so the model carries a genuine survival gate the growth must clear. [13]
- Same-model direct-selling peers (Nu Skin, USANA) trade at rock-bottom multiples on shrinking revenue — evidence the market prices the MLM model itself, not a temporary HLF stumble. [38]
