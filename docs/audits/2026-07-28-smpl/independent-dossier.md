## 1. Variant Perception — Stated, Quantified, Falsifiable

*This field is mandatory. If it cannot be filled with a testable disagreement, there is no position.*

**What the market believes.** That SMPL is a structurally impaired branded-food business losing share in a growing category, whose earnings base has not stopped falling. At $10.28 the market capitalises consensus FY2029 EBITDA of $235.4M at **5.24x**.

**What the market does *not* believe** — and what the audited dossier wrongly assumed it believed — is that the nutritional-snacking category is in decline. It isn't. BellRing's FY2025 annual report describes category growth as robust and expects GLP-1 mainstreaming to *drive* household penetration and category growth; its CEO calls better-for-you RTD shakes one of the fastest-growing CPG categories. SMPL's own CEO says the challenges are execution-driven rather than category-driven. **Any thesis premised on "the market is wrong about GLP-1s" is aimed at a consensus nobody holds.**

### The genuine disagreement is about composition, not level

| | Consensus FY2029 | This dossier, base | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,337.6 | **1,294** | **−3.3%** |
| EBITDA margin | 17.6% | **18.0%** | **+40bp** |
| EBITDA | 235.4 | **232.9** | −1.1% |

**V1 — Revenue is worse than consensus, and the inflection is two years later.** *(bearish, high confidence)*

The arithmetic is mechanical. Atkins is ~25% of sales declining ~25%; Quest is ~65% growing ~2%. Consolidated revenue cannot turn positive until the Atkins drag falls below the Quest contribution, which requires Atkins to shrink to roughly 10% of mix.

| | FY2027 | FY2028 | FY2029 | FY2030 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Consolidated YoY (Quest +4%, Atkins −20%) | −2.3% | −1.2% | −0.3% | **+0.4%** |
| Atkins share of mix | 20.2% | 16.4% | 13.1% | 10.5% |

**Consensus models the inflection in FY2028 (+3.6%). It arrives in FY2030.** Consensus needs Quest above 4% or Atkins decaying far slower than the −24.6% just printed.

**V2 — Margin is better than consensus, because the market is capitalising a commodity trough.** *(bullish, medium confidence)*

FY2026 gross margin of 32.5% is guided down ~375bps and sits ~820bps below the FY2021 peak. That compression is dominated by a record whey spike: whey protein concentrate spot above $11/lb and isolate in the $12 range per USDA, standard whey powder up more than 50% since January, WPC up ~108% and WPI ~139% across two years, with some suppliers sold out for the year. Roughly 7.5% of revenue.

SMPL takes a high-single-digit price increase in September. **If the price is retained while whey mean-reverts, gross margin recovers on two axes simultaneously.**

| Whey reversion from peak | Gross margin | EBITDA on a $1,290M base |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0% | +0bp | +$0M |
| 15% | +112bp | **+$15M** |
| 30% | +225bp | **+$29M** |
| 45% | +338bp | **+$44M** |

Consensus embeds only ~110bp of EBITDA-margin recovery between FY2026 and FY2029. The whey column alone could deliver more than that.

**Net effect.** The two views largely cancel at the EBITDA line. **That is the honest finding: on earnings there is no material edge.** The disagreement is that consensus has the *quality* of the recovery wrong — it models a volume recovery that will not arrive and under-models a margin recovery that plausibly will. That matters for the multiple, because markets pay more for volume than for cost relief on a shrinking base.

**Falsification.** V1 dies if Quest prints ≥+5% for two consecutive quarters. V2 dies if USDA whey benchmarks stay within 10% of current levels through mid-2027, or if the September increase is rolled back.

## 2. The Object — Three Assets, Not One

The single largest structural error in the audited dossier was treating SMPL as one business with one twin. It is three, with different decay rates and different comparables.

Brand weights solved from the disclosed YTD growth rates (total −5.4%, Atkins −22.6%, OWYN −5.5%, Quest +3.4%), anchoring OWYN at ~9.5% of FY2025 sales:

| Brand | FY25 $M | FY25 mix | FY26E $M | FY26 mix | Trend | Twin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Quest** | 869 | 59.9% | 885 | 65.5% | +3.4% YTD → **+1.1% Q3** | BellRing — category-aligned, but ceding share |
| **Atkins** | 444 | 30.6% | 333 | 24.7% | **−24.6% Q3**, penetration 8.5% (−220bp) | Medifast — legacy weight-management, terminal track |
| **OWYN** | 138 | 9.5% | 132 | 9.8% | +3.6% Q3, distribution losses flagged 6–12m | Broken small-cap acquisition |
| **Total** | 1,450.9 | | ~1,350 | | −6.9% | composite |

Two consequences the consolidated view hides:

1. **Atkins is a decaying annuity, not a turnaround.** Household penetration down 220bps in a year and a second consecutive impairment year is not a brand that recovers. Model it as a wasting asset with a loyal residual around $150M.
2. **Quest is the entire thesis and it is decelerating.** +3.4% YTD → +1.1% in Q3, in a category management says grew 10%. That is roughly nine points of share loss. Quest is where the equity value lives and it is going the wrong way.

## 4. Margin Bridge

Off the FY2026 guided EBITDA margin of 16.5% ($222.5M ÷ $1,350M):

| Path | FY26 GM | Price retention | Whey reversion | Mix | Deleverage | FY29 GM | FY29 EBITDA margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 32.5% | +120bp | +60bp | −40bp | −90bp | **33.0%** | 15.0% |
| Base | 32.5% | +220bp | +180bp | −40bp | −70bp | **35.4%** | 18.0% |
| Bull | 32.5% | +300bp | +250bp | −20bp | −50bp | **37.3%** | 19.8% |

**The price-increase break-even.** At 32.5% gross margin and a ~6% realised increase, gross profit is protected until volume falls **15.6%** (break-even elasticity −2.6); revenue turns negative once volume falls **5.7%** (elasticity −0.94). At any realistic elasticity between −1.5 and −2.5 the increase **raises gross profit and lowers revenue** — it helps the leg that doesn't drive the multiple.

**Two adverse precedents.** SMPL's own last pricing cycle: gross margin fell 262bps in FY2022 and a further 164bps in FY2023 before recovering 218bps in FY2024 — margin repair arrived in year three, and that was with an expanding distribution base. And the category leader is moving the other way: BellRing's Q2 FY2026 Premier Protein RTD net sales rose 2.3% on 12% volume growth, roughly ten points of negative price/mix, while facing the same input costs.

**The timing risk is the sharpest single issue.** Industry reporting indicates retail pass-through of the whey shock has been minimal so far, promotional commitments for September and October are already locked, and pricing shifts across the category are not expected until Q1–Q2 2027; commodity costs typically take 12–18 months to reach shelves. **SMPL is pricing four to six months ahead of its category.** That is brand-level elasticity, not category-level — the worst configuration, and the reason the bear column above assumes only 120bp of retention.

## 11. What Would Make Me Wrong

**On the bull side.** Quest re-accelerates to mid-single digits — the category is growing 10% and Quest's problems are self-inflicted, so a distribution or innovation fix could close the gap quickly. Chips consumption at +17% and milkshakes near +50% from a small base show the innovation engine is not dead. If Quest gets to +6% while whey reverts, the bull column is live and the stock doubles.

**On the bear side.** The failure mode I am most likely to be underweighting is **non-linear distribution loss**. Elasticity modelling assumes a smooth volume response. Retail shelf resets are not smooth: if velocity drops below threshold after the September increase, SKUs get delisted in the Jan–Mar 2027 reset, and that is permanent and not recoverable by reversing price. Atkins is already losing distribution; OWYN losses are flagged. A price increase taken ahead of the category, into declining velocity, is exactly the setup that triggers a reset cull. That scenario looks like the Severe column, not the Bear column.

**On the framework.** My base multiple of 8.0x is the single largest judgment in this document. Every magnitude — the $12.69 PV, the 1.36:1 ratio, the $9.50 limit — moves proportionally with it. The *direction* of the findings (composition disagreement, not level; re-rating trade, not earnings trade; duration mismatch against a 180-day book) does not depend on it.

## 13. Decision

**No entry at $10.28.** The time-adjusted reward:risk of 1.36:1 does not clear the fund's ratified 2:1 bar, and the shortfall is not a rounding matter — it is the difference between a thesis that pays over 2.5 years and a downside that arrives in one quarter.

**The position becomes actionable on either of two conditions:**

1. **Price:** entry at **≤$9.50**, which restores 2:1 against the probability-weighted present value.
2. **Evidence:** Quest ≥ +3% year-over-year for two consecutive quarters **and** initial FY2027 guidance ≥ $1.30B. Either alone is insufficient — the first without the second means Quest is growing into an Atkins hole; the second without the first means the guide is not credible.

**Sizing if entered:** half-weight maximum. Single reportable segment, one brand carrying the equity value, 8.2% short interest, ~2.9-day days-to-cover liquidity.

**Do not act before the October print.** There is no informational advantage available between now and then, and the print tests three of the ten kill criteria simultaneously.
