- **P0 · one fuel-cell thesis** (`engine._identity_differences`, `_earlier_match`). Two readings of
  ONE article that rest on the SAME evidence — at least `SAME_EVIDENCE_SHARE` (two thirds) of EACH
  side's cited sentences shared — are one thesis even when the readings name mechanisms of
  different families: one causal chain read at two steps. The earlier id and mechanism are kept;
  the other label is stated with what each step means for earnings (`FAMILY_CONSEQUENCE`). With
  evidence of its own on either side, the reading may be a second, separately falsifiable
  mechanism: created, held for review, told both consequences. Not a rule for all share / volume
  pairs — it reads the evidence. Real: the cleaned fuel-cell reading (14 of its 17 sentences on
  the first thesis; 14 of the thesis's 19 back) now UPDATES `TH-DDEE0B7EF0` (v2, WATCH kept,
  history v1 → v2, the scandium/SOFC risk carried); no `TH-F14CB7F84C` beside it.
- **P0 · a thesis's segment is its economic subject** (`engine.subject_segment`, taxonomy
  `grid_equipment`). In order: (1) the subject the thesis statement names; (2) the segment of its
  product, or of the sectors the source names as exposed; (3) segments at least
  `PRODUCT_MIN_SHARE` (a fifth) of its verified quotes name — never one an incidental comparison
  names once; unassigned rather than wrong. New segment: transformers / grid equipment
  (transformers, switchgear, substations, grid / high-voltage / electrical equipment, GOES). A
  thesis a later reading reaches by ANOTHER key, whose own segment under today's rule differs
  from its key's, is RE-KEYED (`_correct_segment`, `Store.rekey_thesis`): the same id, versions
  and evidence; `KEY_CORRECTED` event with the old key; never onto a key another thesis holds
  (`KEY_NOT_CORRECTED` for review); a former key still finds its thesis (`_by_key`), so it can
  never be re-created over it. Comparisons use the segment AS IT IS TODAY on both routes; a
  re-derived "none" is no evidence of a shared subject (`_same_subject`).
- **A latent absorption hazard closed** (`_same_article_thesis`): a thesis with NO segment nests
  with every segment, so one of an article's stories on the same node, mechanism and place could
  fold another into it. When the segments differ, the earlier thesis must now hold one of the
  hypothesis's sentences (a preview → full text always does). Found on the real re-read: the
  transformer reading was briefly matched to the skilled-labour thesis (its re-derived segment
  none) — the verification-hold invariant refused the resulting promotion, loudly.
