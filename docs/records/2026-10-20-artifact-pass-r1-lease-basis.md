# Artifact pass: R1's one lease basis lands on the committed pages — 24 carry their lease basis, 16 anchors re-derived

**WHAT.** The pages side of #750, on main `618f07e5` (which includes #751). Artifacts only:
`data/dossiers/*` and their `frontend/dossiers/*` mirror, two EDGAR fixtures, this record and STATE.
- **Step 1, `refresh.lease_basis_transform`** through `refresh_dossier` (kept synthesis) on all 27
  committed pages. 24 now carry `valuation.lease_basis`:
  - 16 material with an ex-lease history: AMZN, AORT, BURL, CNXC, EPAM, NCNO, NFLX, NKE, PARR,
    ROST, SMPL, TGT, TJX, VITL, WERN, WLK (NCNO is an R2 page with no R1 multiple);
  - 2 material and refused: MVST (6 quarters of history, ≥8 needed) and UBER (4 of 30 checkable
    quarters unreconciled);
  - 6 immaterial: BRBR, GOOGL, IT, LLY, V, VRRM.
  - FUBO, ORKA and TECX were left alone by the transform's own rule: they carry no CIQ EV/EBITDA
    history, so R1 has nothing to price on either basis.
- **Step 2, `anchor-backfill`** on the lease-material pages only (MVST excepted, below): 16 anchors
  re-derived, UBER's unchanged. Immaterial names were not backfilled: on LLY a backfill moves a
  `vendor_bridge` flag for reasons unrelated to leases.
- **Two hand corrections, one field each:**
  - **AMZN's anchor reason** is restored verbatim. The backfill replaced the operator's R4
    re-tag text with the machine's, under the existing `preserve_operator_tag` rule that refreshes
    a reason whenever the recipe is unchanged. #701 restored this exact field after #700 did the
    same. The rule is now an open item (STATE §5.0 row 27 (5)).
  - **MVST's page** is main's plus step 1 only. A backfill would have moved its net debt from none
    to CIQ's $65.2M (`vendor_bridge`) — the backfill passes CIQ's bridge and assembly does not. That
    has nothing to do with leases (MVST's lease basis refuses, so R1's path is unchanged).
- **Fixtures:** `tests/fixtures/companyfacts/CNXC.json` and `NKE.json` regenerated by
  `scripts/build_companyfacts_fixture.py` from the same cache the backfill read (its own rule: the
  fixture tracks the artifacts). Those are the two names whose anchors also moved on newer EDGAR
  facts; every other name's recompute already agreed.

**WHY.** #750 changed R1 to price lease-material names on one basis; until this pass the committed
pages still carried anchors on CIQ's mixed history. The first build of this pass (2026-10-19, on
`4bba300c`) turned the suite red and found a #750 defect — the freshness gate read the ex-lease
band's date as an export arrival, and AMZN's page printed "oldest panel 70d old". #751 fixed it;
this pass was rebuilt from scratch on main with #751, and AMZN's page now reads "3d old — 2026-10-06".
