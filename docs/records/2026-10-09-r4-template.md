# The R4 template — an R4-tagged page gets the recipe's own shape, its bridge comes from the page, and UBER is no longer refused by R1's applicable bit

**WHAT.** `scenario-propose` on a page tagged `R4_forward_ev_ebit` emits an R4-shaped template
(`propose.build_r4_template`). Every other page keeps its template unchanged.

| part | what it is |
|---|---|
| recipe, basis | `R4_forward_ev_ebit`, `EV/NTM EBIT` |
| leg cells | `ebit` and `multiple`, each EMPTY with its reason and a required `*_derivation`; the base leg's `years_to_terminal` and `leg_fy`; the bear leg's `bear_basis` |
| `net_debt` fact | the page's `ev_bridge` exit claims: CIQ's TEV claims less market cap (Net Debt + preferred + minority), held to exit by the operator's rule, with the build-up and both export hashes in its basis and source |
| `diluted_shares` fact | `resolve_shares`, as R1 |
| evidence | EBIT: consensus by FY with dispersion, its 18-month path, the reported arc, the final consensus's miss per year, every guidance row. Multiple: today's EV/EBIT, the page's own EV/NTM EBIT band, the peer row. Bridge: every line, its checks, the consensus test, the exit claims, the rule |

No revenue, margin or EPS cell is emitted, and no D&A fact: `_run_r4` refuses all four. The
EV/EBITDA basis it refuses is gone too.

**The template REFUSES, rather than prefills:**
- **No bridge on the page:** FUBO, ORKA and TECX today. The bridge is checked first, so FUBO is
  told this rather than the share-count disagreement it also carries.
- **An unchecked or flagged bridge:** no CIQ TEV less market cap, or one more than
  `EV_BRIDGE_CIQ_TOL` away from it. That tolerance is now ONE named constant in `ciq_sheets`,
  read by both the bridge's `DISAGREES` note and this refusal.
- **A bridge and a multiple from two pulls:** the bridge's Estimates hash is not the
  `forward_consensus` one.
- **Exit claims that differ by fiscal year:** the template cannot choose the record's exit year.
  No committed page carries this case; every exit is held.

**The tier gate's exception for R4.** `check_tier_agreement` refused any page whose
`applicable` is False. That bit is R1's: whether R1's arithmetic can anchor. R4's eligibility,
ruled 2026-10-08, *includes* "R1 cannot anchor", so on an R4-tagged page it is the expected state.
UBER carries it, and would have been refused by its own eligibility condition. The exception is
R4's alone: R3's coupling (§5.0 row 10, #670) is unchanged and pinned.

**WHY.** The next step in the ruled build order (STATE row 10). Operator instruction, 2026-10-09:
"start the R4 template". Before this, an R4 page got the R1 shape: a pre-filled revenue, margin
and D&A fact and an EV/EBITDA basis, all four refused by `_run_r4`. An R4 record could only be
written by hand-deleting half the template, the hand-correction row 10 records for R3, one
recipe worse.

**WHAT IS NOT PROPOSED, and why:**
- **NTM EBIT at exit** is the recipe's whole leg driver. Proposing it would be the machine writing
  the underwrite, which is R3's EPS rule.
- **The multiple has no ruled anchor.** R1's base anchor is a TRAILING EV/EBITDA median, a
  different multiple, and no ruling makes the page's EV/NTM EBIT history its R4 counterpart.
  - The page shows why a default would mislead. On AMZN, today's 22.59x sits BELOW its own
    nine-year low (23.22x), and the median is 52.56x.
  - So a median default would propose 2.3× today's multiple, struck on years when the EBIT it
    priced was a fraction of today's.
  - The empty cell's reason states this from the band.
- **The base leg's `leg_fy`.** R1 pre-fills the EV-gap's year because its revenue anchor sits
  there, by construction. R4 proposes no EBIT, so there is no year the proposal already chose.
