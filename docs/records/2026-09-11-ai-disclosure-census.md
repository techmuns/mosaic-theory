# The AI card's confirming test cannot be read on any of its nine names — a disclosure census

**WHAT.** The first-confirming asks whether *"the disclosed model-agnostic AI monetization line
GROWS"* at one representative per class. **Censused against primary filings: ZERO of the nine
disclose such a line.** On 2026-12-15 there is nothing to read. **The nearest readable construct is
RPO CONVERSION, and it covers two of the three classes — not silicon.**

Sizing before valuation, applied to a card: census the disclosure before proposing a trigger on it.

## Population and method

The nine the card names: **MSFT / GOOGL / AMZN** (hyperscaler), **CRM / NOW / VEEV / SAP / META**
(app-layer), **NVDA** (silicon). **Latest annual + latest quarterly primary document each, read in
full — 18 of 18 documents, 881,731 words** — not the driver-exposure extracts, which cannot establish
absence.

## AI-ATTRIBUTED REVENUE: 0 of 9

Four hits across all eighteen documents, and **not one is a disclosure of the filer's own AI
revenue**:

- **MSFT** (both docs) — a **risk factor about competitors**: *"companies compete with us by…
  using AI models that are open, and earning revenue on advertising or integrated products"*.
- **NVDA** annual — the opposite of a disclosure: *"it is difficult to estimate with any reasonable
  degree of precision **the impact of accelerated computing and AI models on our reported
  revenue**"*. **The silicon representative states the attribution is not estimable.**
- **NVDA** quarterly — a conditional future arrangement: *"If certain criteria are met, we will
  participate in revenue share generated by the AI clouds"*.

**The trigger as written is unreadable on 2026-12-15**, and this is now evidenced rather than
suspected. Row 22 previously recorded that the committed corpus *could not confirm* such a line is
published; against primary filings, it is not.

## RPO CONVERSION: 5 of 9, and the gap is CLASS-SHAPED

Framed as **conversion, not accumulation** — a growing backlog satisfies the thesis and its null at
once, so the readable quantity is the share recognised in the next twelve months.

**Coverage by the trigger's own unit — one representative per class:**

| class | readable? | on whom |
|---|---|---|
| hyperscaler | **YES** | MSFT (a stated 30% rate) |
| app-layer | **YES** | CRM, NOW (46%), VEEV |
| **silicon** | **NO** | NVDA has no conversion line, and says the attribution is not estimable |

**So a conversion re-cut is available for two of three classes and fails on the third.** The silicon
leg needs a different line or must leave the confirming test.

**Two readability constraints, both dated-trigger problems:**

- **SAP's quarterly primary document is a 526-word COVER PAGE.** As a foreign private issuer it files
  a 6-K, not a 10-Q; the substance sits in an exhibit. **A trigger readable on a fixed date cannot
  rely on SAP's quarterly filing.**
- **AMZN and META disclose no RPO at all** — so neither can ever carry its class.

**PAID-SEAT COUNTS: 3 of 9** (MSFT, SAP, VEEV) — thin, and set aside on the prior ruling that seats
are the unit abundance removes, so a rising count is ambiguous rather than confirming.

## What this does NOT do

**No trigger is proposed.** The card has had two proposals rejected — cloud segment operating margin
(an input-cost proxy moving on depreciation and capex timing, confirming cleanly and disconfirming
weakly) and the original line — and the lesson was to establish the disclosure first. **This record
establishes it. The re-cut is the operator's**, and the census says what is available to cut onto.

**BOUNDARY.** Population: the nine representatives named in `data/themes/thesis/ai-value-migration.json`
`first_confirming`, resolved to CIKs via EDGAR `company_tickers.json`. Documents: the latest annual
(10-K ×8, 20-F ×1) and latest quarterly (10-Q ×8, 6-K ×1) **primary document** per name, fetched live
from EDGAR 2026-09-11; filing dates 2026-01-29 → 2026-08-27; **18 of 18 read, 881,731 words total**,
zero errors. Vocabularies, ≥2 per concept: RPO = `remaining performance obligation` / `backlog` /
`current remaining performance obligation|cRPO`; conversion = `(expect|anticipate)…recogniz…(next
twelve months|next 12 months|following 12|within one year)`; AI revenue = `AI…revenue` /
`artificial intelligence…revenue` / `revenue…AI`; products = `Copilot|Gemini|Einstein|Now
Assist|Bedrock|SageMaker|Joule|Vertex AI|Azure AI|Llama`; seats = `seats|paid customers|subscription
seats`. Basket membership from `data/themes/baskets/ai-value-migration.json`, freeze 2026-09-01.

**WHAT IT COSTS / WHAT IS ACCEPTED — checked, not assumed.** **This censuses PRIMARY DOCUMENTS ONLY.**
An AI revenue line could exist in an earnings deck, a call, or an 8-K exhibit and would not appear
here — which is exactly how WAT's 40% lived on a call while its 10-Q reported blended. **That is a
real limit and it is the right one for THIS question**: a first-confirming read on a fixed date must
be readable in a filing that lands on that date, and a deck figure is not. **The cheap extension was
considered and rejected as answering a different question.** **Falsifier: any of the nine disclosing
an AI-attributed revenue line in a primary filing** — at which point the original trigger becomes
readable and no re-cut is needed. **Second accepted item: `rate stated` is read from the conversion
sentence only**; CRM, SAP and VEEV may quantify conversion elsewhere in their RPO notes, so "—" there
means *not stated in the conversion sentence*, not *absent from the filing*.
