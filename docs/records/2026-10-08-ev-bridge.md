# The EV bridge is CIQ's current TEV build-up — it equals CIQ's own TEV less market cap on all 26 names, and the first cut was wrong on UBER

**WHAT.** A new dossier-level field, `ev_bridge`: the claims between CIQ's enterprise value and
equity — what an enterprise recipe (R4) subtracts from TEV — with the exit default the operator's
rule sets. One builder, `ciq_sheets.ev_bridge`, serves assembly and `ev_bridge_transform`.

| part | what it is |
|---|---|
| `claims` | CIQ's CURRENT TEV build-up less market cap: Net Debt + preferred + minority interest, every line read at ONE dated balance-sheet column, the latest. This is how CIQ's comps sheet builds TEV |
| `lines` | Total Debt, Total Cash & ST Investments, what CIQ's Net Debt nets beyond cash (long-term marketable securities, where that column discloses them), preferred, minority interest |
| `net_debt` | CIQ's balance-sheet 'Net Debt' (the comps figure) |
| `ciq_tev_claims` | CIQ's own current TEV less market cap, from the comps export — the check the bridge must pass; the page says so where it does not |
| `edgar_net_debt` | the anchor's figure, as a cross-check |
| `consensus` | CIQ's consensus 'Net Debt' line, tested year by year against the balance sheet |
| `exit_claims` | the default claims at each fiscal year-end, with the method used |

The field sits outside `mechanical`, ABSENT until built, before `anchor`. Version row `2026-10-08.4`
and its JS twin.

**WHY.** Step (3), part two, of the ruled build order: the bridge facts an R4 template reads, with
no manual input (`2026-10-08-r4-forward-ev-ebit.md`).

The operator ruled "CIQ net debt — same source as the multiple, so the bridge cannot mismatch it".
Taken at its reason, that is the claims CIQ's TEV actually carries. CIQ's 'Net Debt' line alone
misses preferred and minority interest, and the two differ on **10 of 26 names**:

| name | claims ($M) | Net Debt ($M) | difference |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GOOGL | −103,660 | −121,683 | preferred +18,023 |
| FUBO | 1,998 | 172 | minority +1,826 |
| UBER | 10,423 | 9,340 | minority +1,083 |
| V | 10,580 | 10,066 | preferred +514 |
| WLK | 4,527 | 4,024 | minority +503 |
| PARR / WERN / NCNO / ORKA / EPAM | | | ≤ $37M each |

AMZN's are identical ($128,647M).

**THE FIRST CUT WAS WRONG ON UBER, AND ITS CHECK COULD NOT SEE IT (diagnosed 2026-10-09).**

*What it did.* It built the claims from total debt, cash, preferred and minority at the latest
balance-sheet column. It then subtracted long-term marketable securities from Historical
Capitalization "at their last reported period", after that sheet's footnote. UBER's latest column
is a Q2 press release that discloses no such securities, so the first cut subtracted Q1's $3,873M.

*What CIQ does.* CIQ's current TEV subtracts nothing there: its 'Net Debt' nets the securities only
where the column discloses them.
- The comps sheet's UBER TEV − market cap is **$10,423M**.
- Backed out of the Estimates multiple itself, at TEV/Revenue × the consensus revenue on the same
  sheet, less price × shares, it is about $10,410M.
- The first cut read **$6,550M**. On an R4 underwrite that overvalues UBER by $3,873M — about
  $1.89 a share, roughly 2.8% of its price.

*Why its check missed it.* The first cut's "26 of 26" check rebuilt CIQ's TEV less market cap on
Historical Capitalization's own FILING-dated columns, where the securities are always disclosed.
It validated the formula at a vintage the multiple does not use. The check now compares against
CIQ's CURRENT figure, the quantity the multiple embeds.

**ONE MATCHING YEAR WAS A COINCIDENCE (diagnosed 2026-10-09).** The first cut called a consensus
net-debt line same-basis on its last reported year alone. NKE passed at 0.09% and set its exit from
broker forecasts. Year by year, NKE's line matched the balance sheet in FY2017 (0.00%) and FY2026
(0.09%), and missed by 22–28% in each of FY2020–25. That is consistent with brokers' net debt
leaving out the leases on the balance sheet since 2019 — an inference from the pattern, not
itemized. A line is now same-basis only when each of its last three reported years agrees within
0.5%. **No committed name passes, so every exit holds today's claims.**

**THE EXIT DEFAULT, by the operator's rule.** A reliable same-basis consensus is used when all of
these hold, with preferred and minority held:
- each of its last three reported years agrees with the balance sheet within 0.5%;
- the newest of them is the latest completed year;
- it carries forward values.

Otherwise today's claims are held. Never current − FCF; no manual input. On the 26: none passes
three years — the nearest are LLY (1.6–2.6%), BRBR (0.8–2.7%), VRRM (2.8–3.3%) and V (3.5–3.9%) —
and MVST's line can be checked on one year only (FY2021).

**BOUNDARY.**
- **Population:** the 26 committed dossiers at main @bd5c2ee6, and each one's on-disk Financials,
  Estimates and comps exports.
- **Dates:** first built 2026-10-08; corrected and re-measured 2026-10-09.
- **Units:** $M.
- **"Exactly":** within $0.5M.
- **Gap ratios:** |reported − balance-sheet Net Debt| ÷ the larger of |net debt| and total debt at
  that year-end.

**WHAT IT COSTS / WHAT IS ACCEPTED.**
- **The bridge and the multiple can still sit on different balance sheets** if a filing lands
  between the Financials, Estimates and comps pulls. Today they agree on every name. The page now
  flags any disagreement with CIQ's current figure; the R4 template must not prefill from a flagged
  bridge.
- **THE STALE-ROW READ — DIAGNOSED 2026-10-09, NARROWER THAN FIRST STATED.**
  - **Where it came from.** UBER's "Total Pref. Equity" read $14,224M (its pre-IPO preferred)
    through `ciq_sheets._bs_latest`, which takes a row's rightmost NON-EMPTY cell. That read was
    this build's own exploratory call; no production reader reads that row.
  - **It is a documented design.** `_latest_col` returns the period beside the value. `key_ratios`
    pins one column, and `free_cash_flow`'s basis note forces CFO and capex onto its column (#421).
  - **Measured across every consumer on the 26 exports.** 5 reads on 2 names take an older column:
    - four are already withheld on the page: FUBO's ROA, RoCE and RoE ("NA"), BRBR's RoE ("NM");
    - one, FUBO's Levered FCF, shows −$107.8M with its true period (FY Sep-27-2025).

    The one real effect is FUBO's prose calling that figure "LTM", which is a synthesis mislabel.
    This bridge reads every line at one dated column, so the class cannot reach it.
- **FUBO, ORKA and TECX wait for a re-assembly to carry the field.** No structured field on their
  pages names the Financials export: they have no own-history band, and the hash appears only
  inside display strings. Each does name its Estimates export. None can use R4 today — none has an
  NTM EV/EBIT multiple, and each page's recipe hint is `unknown` — so the gap costs R4 nothing.
  Relaxing the gate to the Estimates export alone would risk a newer balance sheet under an older
  page.
- **The R4 template is next.** `scenario-propose` reads `exit_claims` for the record's exit year.
