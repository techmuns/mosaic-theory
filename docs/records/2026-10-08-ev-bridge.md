# The EV bridge is CIQ's own TEV build-up — it reproduces CIQ's TEV less market cap on all 26 names, and differs from "CIQ net debt" on 11

**WHAT.** A new dossier-level field, `ev_bridge`: the claims between CIQ's enterprise value and
equity — what an enterprise recipe (R4) subtracts from TEV — with the exit default the operator's
rule sets. One builder, `ciq_sheets.ev_bridge`, serves assembly and `ev_bridge_transform`.

| part | what it is |
|---|---|
| `lines` | CIQ's TEV build-up, read at ONE dated balance-sheet column: Total Debt (+), Total Pref. Equity (+), Total Minority Interest (+), Total Cash & ST Investments (−) |
| long-term marketable securities (−) | from Financials→Historical Capitalization, at its last reported period — the one build-up line the balance sheet does not carry. CIQ's own footnote states the rule: a period with no disclosed value uses the last reported one |
| `claims` | the signed sum, = CIQ's TEV less market cap |
| `net_debt` | Total Debt − Total Cash & ST Investments (CIQ's "Net Debt", the comps figure), shown beside |
| `edgar_net_debt` | the anchor's figure, as a cross-check |
| `consensus` | CIQ's consensus "Net Debt" line tested for basis |
| `exit_claims` | the default claims at each fiscal year-end, with the method used |

The field sits outside `mechanical`, ABSENT until built, before `anchor`. Version row `2026-10-08.4`
and its JS twin.

**WHY.** Step (3), part two, of the ruled build order: the bridge facts an R4 template reads, with
no manual input (`2026-10-08-r4-forward-ev-ebit.md`).

**THE OPERATOR RULED "CIQ net debt — same source as the multiple, so the bridge cannot mismatch
it". Taken at its reason, that is CIQ's TEV build-up, not its "Net Debt" line.** CIQ prints the
build-up:

> TEV = market cap − cash & ST investments + total debt + preferred + minority interest −
> long-term marketable securities

"Net Debt" equals the claims only where the last three lines are nil. On 2026-10-08 they differ on
**11 of 26 names**:

|---|---:|---:|---|
| UBER | 6,550 | 9,340 | minority +1,083; LT marketable securities −3,873 |
| GOOGL | −103,660 | −121,683 | preferred +18,023 |
| FUBO | 1,998 | 172 | minority +1,826 |
| LLY | 45,162 | 45,958 | LT marketable securities −796 |
| V | 10,580 | 10,066 | preferred +514 |
| WLK | 4,527 | 4,024 | minority +503 |
| PARR / WERN / NCNO / EPAM / ORKA | — | — | ≤ $252M each |

AMZN's are identical ($128,647M). **This reading is flagged for the operator's confirmation.**
Every component is on the page, so the "Net Debt" figure stays visible beside it.

**THE EXIT DEFAULT, by the operator's rule.** A reliable same-basis consensus is used when its
last reported year matches CIQ's balance sheet within 0.5%, that year is the latest completed one,
and it carries forward values. Its forward net debt then stands in, with the other claims held.
Otherwise today's claims are held. Never current − FCF.

On the 26:
- **NKE** is the one reliable line: its reported FY2026 sits 0.09% from the balance sheet, so its
  exit is by consensus ($1,724M / $2,160M / $2,229M, FY2027–29).
- **MVST** matches (0.31%) but stopped at FY2021, so it is held.
- **The next-nearest current lines miss by 2.6–5.3%:** BRBR, LLY, VRRM, V, WERN.
- **The rest miss by 6.9% or more.** AMZN's line reports −$57,381M for FY2025 against +$55,518M on
  CIQ's balance sheet.

**HOW VERIFIED.**
- **The independent check.** Historical Capitalization prints CIQ's TEV and market cap per filing.
  Rebuilt from the balance-sheet rows at the quarter that filing priced, plus its long-term
  marketable securities, the claims equal CIQ's TEV − market cap **exactly on 26 of 26 names**.
  The build-up is CIQ's, not a construction of this repo.
- **Real run on all 26.** The claims are as tabled. UBER's long-term marketable securities are its
  Q1 figure beside a Q2 press-release balance sheet, dated as such on the line.
- **52/53-week year-ends match within seven days.** SMPL's year ends Aug-30 and VITL's Dec-28.
  Exact matching found no balance-sheet column for either.
- **Dry run through `refresh_dossier`** on copies of all 26 pages:

**BOUNDARY.**
- **Population:** the 26 committed dossiers at main @bd5c2ee6, and each one's on-disk Financials
  and Estimates exports.
- **Date:** measured 2026-10-08.
- **Units:** $M.
- **"Exactly":** within $0.5M.
- **Gap ratios:** |reported − balance sheet| ÷ the larger of |net debt| and total debt at that
  year-end.

**WHAT IT COSTS / WHAT IS ACCEPTED.**
- **A READER HAZARD, FOUND AND NOT FIXED HERE.** `ciq_sheets._bs_latest` returns a row's rightmost
  NON-EMPTY cell, so a line that stopped being reported returns an old value as the latest. UBER's
  "Total Pref. Equity" read $14,224M that way — its pre-IPO preferred — while the latest quarter
  carries none. This bridge reads every line at ONE dated column, so it is immune. The existing
  `_bs_latest` consumers read continuously reported rows ("Net Debt", "Total Debt"); whether any
  reads a row that can stop (leases, for one) is not checked here. Reported on §5.0 row 27.
- **FUBO, ORKA and TECX** wait for a re-assembly to carry the field.
- **The R4 template is next.** `scenario-propose` reads `exit_claims` for the record's exit year.
- **Long-term marketable securities lag where the balance sheet runs ahead** (UBER: Q1 beside Q2).
  That is CIQ's rule mirrored, and the line says so.
