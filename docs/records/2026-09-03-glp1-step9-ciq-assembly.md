# GLP-1 step 9 — the CIQ pull, assembled; and the premium half does not rank as the shortlist assumed

**BOUNDARY.** Source: the operator's CIQ exports for WST, WAT, BDX, MCK (Estimates Report +
Company Comparable Analysis), comp-set as-of ~2026-09-02. Read 2026-09-03. **No raw CIQ content is
reproduced here or committed** — these are derived analytical values only, per the licensing rule.
Units: USD, EPS Normalized (Capital IQ consensus), P/E on Capital IQ's NTM forward basis.

## Forward consensus — and the fiscal years do NOT line up

| | fiscal year end | FY2026E | FY2027E | FY2028E | coverage FY27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **WST** | Dec-31 | 8.96 | 9.96 | 11.34 | 16/16 |
| **WAT** | Dec-31 | 14.58 | 16.55 | 18.54 | 24/24 |
| **BDX** | **Sep-30** | 12.62 | 13.29 | 14.18 | 14/14 |
| **MCK** | **Mar-31** | 39.11 | 44.62 | 50.46 | 17/17 |

**BDX closes September and MCK closes March, so "FY2027" is a different calendar window for each
name.** MCK's FY2027 ends 31 Mar 2027 — under seven months out — while WST's and WAT's run to 31 Dec
2027. Any cross-name comparison on a fiscal-year basis is invalid without restating to a common
window; **only NTM is comparable as published.** This is the `MC4` hazard (the CIQ parser off
December fiscal years) showing up in the first pull that needed it.

## The premium half, now measurable — and it inverts the shortlist's premise

The shortlist prong is **materiality WITHOUT premium**. Materiality was established at step 8. This
is the other half, on NTM forward P/E against each name's own CIQ comp set:

| | NTM fwd P/E | peer median | **premium** | peers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **WST** | 36.94 | 24.89 | **+48%** | 10 |
| **MCK** | 19.87 | 16.68 | +19% | **4** |
| **WAT** | 26.39 | 23.73 | +11% | 10 |
| **BDX** | 14.17 | 17.19 | **−18%** | 10 |

**WST — the shortlist's largest materiality position at 18% of net sales — is the most
premium-rated of the four by a wide margin.** The analyst note's framing, that the quantified GLP-1
economics *"sit in names carrying no theme premium"*, does not hold for WST on this measure. BDX is
the only one of the four at a discount to its peers.

**THE CAVEAT IS LOAD-BEARING AND THIS PULL CANNOT RESOLVE IT.** A peer-relative premium is not the
same as a **theme** premium. WST is a high-margin consumables franchise that may have traded above
Charles River / Bruker / Avantor long before GLP-1 existed; +48% today says nothing about whether it
**re-rated on the driver**. Answering that needs a multiple TIME SERIES, and these exports are
point-in-time comp sets. **So the finding is: the premium exists and is large; whether it is a GLP-1
premium is unresolved.** Recorded as an open question, not as a conclusion about the shortlist.

**MCK's comp set is 4 names against 10 for the others** — a thinner basis, so its +19% is the least
reliable figure in the table.

## Verification

The NTM P/E figures were cross-checked against a **second, independent CIQ file** — the Estimates
Report `Multiples` sheet — and agree exactly for all four (36.94 / 26.39 / 14.17 / 19.87). That
check caught a real error: a first pass matched the subject company by name prefix, and because
Waters sits inside West's comp set it returned **WAT's multiple as WST's and vice versa**. Two
sources agreeing is what surfaced it; one source would have shipped a transposed premium ranking.

## What is still owed before an underwrite can be written

1. **A multiple time series per name**, to separate a standing quality premium from a driver
   re-rating. Without it the "without premium" prong cannot be adjudicated.
2. **The basis-match judgement** each underwrite requires — whether the comp set's P/E shares the
   normalized denominator the legs will use. GOOGL's record labels its own quartile basis **LIMITED**
   and treats it as context rather than load-bearing; the same call is owed here per name.
3. **The non-filing `source_ref` stamps.** WAT's ~40% GLP-1 testing-line growth is a Q2-26 **earnings
   call** disclosure and its 10-Q reports blended — the transcript is in the pull
   (`Waters Corporation, Q2 2026 Earnings Call, Aug 04, 2026`) and must be cited as the source, not
   the 10-Q.
4. **The legs themselves are the analyst's act.** Nothing here proposes bear/base/bull EPS or
   multiples; consensus is an input to that judgement, never a substitute for it.
