- **The queue:** 141 names entered, 87 left and 202 stayed.
- **Tail steps:**

**WHY.** Operator instruction, 2026-10-17, choosing item 2 of the open list: the queue had not
advanced since 2026-09-22. The weekly screen cannot run in CI on the current Polygon plan, so it is
a local job (STATE §5.0 row 31).

**HOW IT RAN, AND WHAT IT COST.**
- **The store.** A SQLite-backup copy of this machine's `data/vinci.db`, taken from the primary
  checkout on a read-only connection. That store holds 1,204 sessions of the 1,825-day window,
  through 2026-08-05. The run took it to 1,255, through 2026-10-16, adding 109,201 price rows.
  - The primary checkout's store is unchanged; its md5 was taken before the copy.
  - The caches (`polygon`, `sec`, `efts`) were APFS clones into this worktree, so nothing was
    written there either.
- **Wall clock: 6.39 h** (15:23:48Z → 21:46:38Z, 2026-10-17), against 53 minutes for the
  2026-09-23 run.
  - The difference is the universe build's per-name share-count lookups: 1,104 Polygon
    `/v3/reference/tickers/<T>` calls at the plan's measured ~4 per minute (about 4.6 h). The
    2026-09-23 run's machine had most of them in its netcache; this machine's cache dates from
    August.
  - The run also made 111 grouped-daily calls and read 14 ticker-list pages, and wrote 4,772 SEC
    cache files. That makes 1,229 Polygon cache entries written during the run.
  - **A cold-cache local run therefore costs about 6.5 hours**, not the 53 minutes the last
    record measured on a warm one.
- **No model calls.** The weekly path is Polygon and EDGAR only (traced through every module
  `vinci/orchestrator.py` imports).
