# COR and A settled from EDGAR at zero marginal cost — the leg closes at WST alone

**WHAT.** The prior record called these two *"one CIQ pull each"*. **That reached for the wrong
instrument.** Both were answerable from filings already in `data/cache/sec/`, and both are now
answered. **COR is GATED on the strongest disclosure of any name in this theme.** **A carries no
GLP-1 figure in any of six vocabularies across 119,719 words of filings.** The picks-and-shovels leg
closes at **WST alone**.

## COR — Cencora states that GLP-1 REDUCES its gross margin. Three times, in two filings.

**FY2025 10-K:**

> *"Healthcare Solutions' **gross profit margin of 2.72%** in the current fiscal year increased 30
> basis points compared to the prior fiscal year primarily due to the January 2025 acquisition of
> RCA, **offset in part by higher sales of GLP-1 products, which have lower gross profit margins**,
> and lower sales of COVID vaccines, which have higher gross profit margins."*

**Q3 FY2026 10-Q, both the quarter and the nine-month period:**

> *"**gross profit margin of 3.17%** in the current year quarter increased 63 basis points… **offset
> in part by higher sales of GLP-1s, which have lower gross profit margins**."*
> *"**gross profit margin of 2.96%** in the current year nine-month period increased 52 basis
> points… **offset in part by higher sales of GLP-1s, which have lower gross profit margins**."*

Beside a revenue line that is large and growing — **$7.7bn / +26.9%** in FY2025, **+$2.3bn / +25.5%**
in the quarter — all *"largely driven by unit volume growth."*

**This is the sharpest disposition in the theme.** MCK's GLP-1 does not reach profit; CAH's *"did not
meaningfully contribute"*; **Cencora's is DILUTIVE — volume growth in the category makes its segment
gross margin worse, and it says so in its own words, unprompted, three times.** The driver's own
signature (volumes UP) runs against it. **COR is gated.**

and Agilent has less on the record than either: BDX at least named the category.
