# The eight tracked pages are re-assembled onto the 2026-09-22 fleet as-of, and two machine proposals went away

**WHAT.** AMZN, BRBR, GOOGL, LLY, NFLX, NKE, PARR and WLK are re-assembled at
`reference_date: 2026-09-22`, the as-of the 2026-09-22 screen run established (#645). Each was a
FULL re-assembly — new LLM draws — because a mechanical refresh cannot do this: `--no-llm` writes
`synthesis: null`, and `_refuse_synthesis_overwrite` refuses that over a committed synthesis. The
R1 anchors were then restored by `anchor-backfill`, the monitor day was cut at the new as-of, and
the frontend mirror rebuilt.

AMZN's new lean reads *"accelerating AWS/backlog and a self-funding operating base, set against a
cash-consuming heavy-capex phase with ROIC drifting down and forward P/E above peers"*; NFLX's
*"fundamentals … are strong, but a -40-point idiosyncratic de-rating with contaminated trailing
earnings and no identified catalyst is unresolved."* Both are `mixed`, and **`mixed` proposes
NOTHING by design** — a two-sided read is the human's call. The proposals did not flip to AVOID;
they withdrew.

**ONE DRAW CANNOT SEPARATE NEW DATA FROM DRAW VARIANCE.** Each name was drawn ONCE here. The inputs
did move — a month of prices, the 2026-09-22 screen window — but nothing in this pass establishes
that a re-draw on the OLD data would have held `strongly-bullish`. Read the two withdrawals as
"the machine no longer proposes", not as "the machine changed its mind about the business."

**WHY.** #645 advanced the served queue four weeks, but every committed page still rendered the
2026-08-24 as-of: the queue advancing does not re-render pages. The operator asked for pages
showing current prices, and this is the only path that produces them.

**WHAT IT COSTS / WHAT IS ACCEPTED.**

1. **Re-rendering a page re-draws its verdict.** There is no cheap path: the refusal that protects a
   committed synthesis from a `--no-llm` build also means every as-of refresh spends LLM draws and
   moves leans. Accepted deliberately; the falsifier is a pass where a lean flips with no change in
   the underlying inputs at all.
2. **Two names lost a BUY proposal on a single draw.** Not re-drawn to confirm. A second draw would
   cost the same again and still not be a control — the honest control is the forward spread, which
   accumulates on the human's verdict of record, not on the machine's proposal.
