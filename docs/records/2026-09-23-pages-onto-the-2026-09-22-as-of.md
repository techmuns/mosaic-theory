# The eight tracked pages are re-assembled onto the 2026-09-22 fleet as-of, and two machine proposals went away

**WHAT.** AMZN, BRBR, GOOGL, LLY, NFLX, NKE, PARR and WLK are re-assembled at
`reference_date: 2026-09-22`, the as-of the 2026-09-22 screen run established (#645). Each was a
FULL re-assembly — new LLM draws — because a mechanical refresh cannot do this: `--no-llm` writes
`synthesis: null`, and `_refuse_synthesis_overwrite` refuses that over a committed synthesis. The
R1 anchors were then restored by `anchor-backfill`, the monitor day was cut at the new as-of, and
the frontend mirror rebuilt.

AMZN's new lean reads *"accelerating AWS/backlog and a self-funding operating base, set against a

* **AMZN's withdrawal DOES NOT REPRODUCE.** Identical inputs produced both labels, and draw 3 names
  the very offset draw 2 promoted — *"free cash flow stays negative through the CapEx cycle"* — then
  reads it as strongly-bullish, exactly as draw 1 did with *"cash cost is the main offset"*. Its
  bear count moved 4 → 6 → 4 across draws. **The BUY→none on this page is draw variance, not
  evidence about the business.**
* **~~NFLX's withdrawal DOES reproduce~~ — WITHDRAWN 2026-09-25, it does not.** On n=2 it read
  `mixed` twice and this record called the change attributable to the as-of moving (its central bear
  fact went from −13.4 points of 180d underperformance to ~−40). **Re-measured at n=5 it is 3–2**
  (`mixed` 3, `strongly-bullish` 2) — roughly two in five re-assemblies publish BUY. The n=2 read
  was under-powered, and the bar it fell short of was already on file:
  `docs/records/2026-09-01-opus5-synthesis-regression.md` requires a measured RATE over ≥5 runs on
  ≥3 names for claims of exactly this kind. Full figures are in PR #647's record (it lands with
  that PR, so it is named rather than cited by path from this branch).
* **Two draws prove instability where they disagree; they do NOT prove stability where they agree.**
  That sentence was right and this record then leaned on the half it does not license. AMZN's label
  is established as unstable (and at n=5 the page's `mixed` is the **1-in-5** outcome); nothing was
  established about NFLX, which is what the n=5 re-measurement went on to overturn.
* **THE COMPARISON WAS ALSO CONFOUNDED.** AMZN, NFLX and PARR sit in three DIFFERENT books, and the
  per-book synthesis prompts are 98.6–98.9% identical — the difference being the ~60 characters
  naming the two competing cases. Name and METHOD varied together, so nothing here could attribute a
  difference to either. Holding AMZN's facts byte-identical and swapping only the frame moves
  bear-point counts from a mean 4.7 (quality) to 3.7 (overshoot), nearly disjoint: **book assignment
  shapes the evidence assembled, so it is a decision-surface input rather than a label.** (Measured
  under PR #647.)

**THE COMMITTED ARTIFACT IS DRAW 2 FOR BOTH — the probe did not get to pick.** Draw 3 was restored
away byte-identically. Selecting whichever draw proposes BUY would be choosing a verdict by its
content, which is the thing the guardrails exist to forbid.

**ROOT CAUSE, FOUND WHILE DIAGNOSING THIS.** `AnthropicLlmClient.extract` (the package's ONLY
`messages.create` call site, `vinci/graph/llm.py:66`) passes model, max_tokens, system, tools,
tool_choice and messages — **no `temperature`, no `top_p`, no seed** — so every LLM phase runs at
the API default of 1.0 and every draw is a fresh sample. Meanwhile `vinci/dossier/assemble.py:131`
reasons about artifact reproducibility as *"the property temperature-0 protects, broken without a
test that would notice"*. **That property is asserted and never implemented, and the test it names
does not exist.** Same class as the netcache comment that described a TTL policy the code did not
implement. Recorded here; fixing it is a decision-path change and belongs in its own PR.

**WHY.** #645 advanced the served queue four weeks, but every committed page still rendered the
2026-08-24 as-of: the queue advancing does not re-render pages. The operator asked for pages
showing current prices, and this is the only path that produces them.

**WHAT IT COSTS / WHAT IS ACCEPTED.**

1. **Re-rendering a page re-draws its verdict.** There is no cheap path: the refusal that protects a
   committed synthesis from a `--no-llm` build also means every as-of refresh spends LLM draws and
   moves leans. Accepted deliberately; the falsifier is a pass where a lean flips with no change in
   the underlying inputs at all.
2. **Two names lost a BUY proposal on a single draw.** Not re-drawn to confirm. A second draw would
   cost the same again and still not be a control — the honest control is the forward spread, which
   accumulates on the human's verdict of record, not on the machine's proposal.
