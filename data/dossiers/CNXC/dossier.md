## The case
_(top points; the full argument is in Analysis)_

_Bull case — the decline is overdone:_
- The business is FCF-positive at $775.6M LTM and buying back stock (shares -5.9% over ~1.7y), consistent with a cash-generative, self-funding model rather than a distressed one. [10]
- S&P-MI credit panel scores the name Overall/Operational/Solvency/Liquidity 4/4/4/4 with a BBB- issuer rating and interest coverage of 4.6x — investment-grade, not a solvency cliff. [11]
- The optical 'shrinking margin' read is a misread: EBITDA margin held ~13% while absolute EBITDA rose from $1,045M (FY23) to $1,275M (LTM) alongside revenue climbing to ~$10.0B — the % dip masks rising absolute profit dollars. [12]
_Bear case — the decline is real:_
- Leverage is high and the basis matters: net-debt/EBITDA is 4.1x on CIQ GAAP EBITDA, but on company-adjusted LTM EBITDA ($368.7M annualized-basis) the ratio is ~14.2x — a real signal that headline leverage understates the burden if adjusted EBITDA is the true earnings power; management itself targets cutting net leverage below 2.6x by end-FY26. [13]
- FY26 forward consensus has been marked down hard over 12 months: GAAP EPS -34%, GAAP net income -36%, EBT(GAAP) -37% — a genuine deterioration in the forward earnings path, not merely optical margin drift. [14]
- Two growth verticals — technology/consumer-electronics and healthcare — each declined ~6% constant-currency in Q1 2026 on lighter-than-expected client volumes, a genuine demand soft-spot beneath the aggregate. [15]
