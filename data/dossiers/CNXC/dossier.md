## The argument

**Evidence balance:** mixed — genuine cash generation and a live mix-upgrade thesis sit against heavy 4.1x leverage, a deep GAAP loss, and sharp consensus cuts.

### Bull case — the decline is overdone
- Forward-looking demand mix is shifting toward higher-value work: signed annual contract value for solutions including AI more than doubled quarter-on-quarter, a leading indicator of revenue quality improvement. [10]
- Growth verticals are live — banking and financial services revenue grew 13% year-over-year in constant currency in Q1 2026, a mechanism supporting the modestly positive consensus revenue path. [10]
- Absolute EBITDA has held and edged up even as the margin % looks stuck at 13% — LTM EBITDA $1,275M vs FY25 $1,266M and FY24 $1,295M, so the level is not collapsing despite the optical margin flatness. [11]
- Guidance-delivery track record is strong — actual ≥ EPS-Normalized guided floor 11/12 resolved and revenue ≥ floor 21/25, with the most recent FQ1 2026 MET. [20]
- The business is FCF-positive and self-funding at the LTM horizon, generating $775.6M of levered free cash flow, which supports the reported buyback and dividend. [21]
- Interest coverage of 4.6x and a BBB- issuer rating with credit-health scores (4/4/4/4) argue the balance sheet is serviceable, not on a solvency cliff. [22]
- On peer comps the name's TEV/EBITDA (4.1x) sits below the peer median (5.3x), but the peer set spans $103M–$32.0B (311x) and is heterogeneous — pose whether that gap is EARNED (name margin 12% vs peer 17%, Debt/EBITDA 3.4 vs 2.0) or RESIDUAL overshoot, not a clean discount. [23]

### Bear case — the decline is real
- Net leverage is heavy at 4.1x net-debt/EBITDA and 7.1x net-debt/OCF, well above the peer-median total-debt/EBITDA of 2.0x — this is a load-bearing, structural (not optical) constraint given the $5,249M net-debt base. [8]

- The consensus earnings path has been cut hard: FY26 EPS (GAAP) revised -34% and EBT (GAAP) -37% from the 12m-ago mark, and target price collapsed (e.g. broker $80→$55, $62→$38) — real deterioration, not a timing artifact. [12]
- ROIC has fallen and stayed low — from ~9% in FY22 to 4.2% LTM — signalling returns below prior levels even as revenue scaled via the Webhelp-era acquisition, a genuine quality erosion not reversed by absolute-EBITDA stability. [25]
- GAAP profitability has turned deeply negative — FY25 net income -$1,279M and LTM -$1,314M, driven by a $1,523M goodwill impairment, so the P/E is NM on a negative current base rather than optically low. [26]
- Short interest is high and rising — 19.5% of shares out, up +18.4% to 11.9M shares (settle 2026-06-30), signalling an active bearish stance. [27]
- The drawdown is idiosyncratic, not market-driven — CNXC -45.4% over 180d while IWM +17.0%, a -62.4% relative gap, and gap-downs cluster on debt-offering and earnings 8-Ks. [7]

### Decisive question
**Is the AI-driven mix shift toward higher-value, tech-enabled work a genuine, margin-accretive revenue-quality upgrade that will de-lever the balance sheet toward the committed sub-2.6x, or is AI a secular volume-destruction threat that erodes the core CX book faster than the new offerings can replace it?**

### …evidence for
- Signed annual contract value for solutions including AI more than doubled quarter-on-quarter, and management closed close to 60 enterprise iX Suite deals including its largest iX Hero contracts with two Fortune 50 companies. [10]
- Consensus revenue is still nudging up (FY26 net +2% over 12m) and absolute LTM EBITDA held at $1,275M, consistent with the new work backfilling. [11]

### …evidence against
- The technology/consumer-electronics and health-care verticals both fell about 6% in Q1 2026 on lighter volumes and shore mix — the exact segments most exposed to automation. [10]
- Consensus FY26 GAAP EPS was cut -34% and EBT (GAAP) -37% over 12m, and ROIC sits at 4.2% LTM — the near-term economics are deteriorating, not inflecting. [12]

### Key unknowns
- Company-adjusted LTM EBITDA and the covenant leverage basis are UNKNOWN — net-debt/company-adjusted-EBITDA and covenant headroom cannot be verified; an opaque disclosure gap on the metric that governs the sub-2.6x deleveraging pledge (bearish risk).

- Securitization Facility ($537M, due 2028-03-20) and the remaining $200M 6.65% notes maturing Aug 2026 — refinance/repay execution against the committed deleveraging path is unresolved.
