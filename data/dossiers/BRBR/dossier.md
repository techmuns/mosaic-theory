## The argument

**Evidence balance:** mixed — category still grows and pricing action is dated, but a real -43% forward-EBITDA reset, rising promo/inflation costs and CEO transition are load-bearing.

### Bull case — the decline is overdone
- Net sales still grew 4% in Q3 2026 with both brands ahead of expectations, so the top line is expanding even as margins compress — a mechanism pointing to a margin-and-mix problem, not a revenue collapse. [10]
- Management announced a double-digit price increase on Premier shakes plus additional powder pricing effective Q1 FY2027 to offset input inflation and rebuild margin, a dated, specific mechanism to reverse the very margin pressure driving the de-rate. [10]

- Premier household penetration reached almost 23% with the highest shake repeat rate in the category in Q3 2026, indicating durable brand equity underpinning the franchise. [10]
- The falling EBITDA margin (FY24 21% → LTM 14%) is a % decline against a still-large absolute base: absolute EBITDA rose from 117 in FY17 to 445 in FY25 before the LTM step-down to 332, so the recent hit is a partial retracement off a structurally higher level, not a return to trough economics. [30]
- The business is FCF-positive on an LTM basis ($201.1M) with interest coverage of 4.2x, so it can service its 3.4x leverage while funding operations rather than facing a solvency wall. [31]
- The company is actively buying back stock, reducing shares ~9.7% over ~1.7 years under a $600M program, so capital return is compounding per-share value while the multiple is depressed. [32]

### Bear case — the decline is real
- EBITDA growth is -27% for the name vs +1% peer median while leverage (Debt/EBITDA 3.5 vs 2.4) is worse — the P/E discount to peers (6.3x vs 21.9x) appears substantially EARNED by inferior growth and higher leverage rather than purely residual overshoot, though the peer set is heterogeneous. [12]

### Decisive question
**Is the margin collapse a transient competition-and-inflation squeeze that the announced FY27 double-digit price increase and cost actions can reverse, or a permanent reset of unit economics as insurgent brands and value-seeking consumers structurally raise the cost of defending share?**

### …evidence for
- Management has announced a double-digit Premier shake price increase plus powder pricing effective Q1 FY2027 explicitly to offset input inflation and support a healthier margin profile over time, with expected elasticity only slightly greater than 1. [10]
- The underlying RTD shake category still grew 8% ahead of the broader food industry and Premier holds ~23% household penetration with the highest repeat rate, suggesting demand and brand equity to carry pricing. [11]

### …evidence against
- Promotional intensity hit 27% of category volumes on promotion in Q2 (up 8pts YoY) with the first buy-rate-per-household decline in 5 years, and management flags the rising cost required to maintain leadership — evidence the competitive cost step-up may be structural. [11]
- Protein-driven commodity inflation is running above initial assumptions with the greatest impact landing in Q4, so the cost base against which any price increase must work is still rising. [11]
