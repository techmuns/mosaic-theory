## Synthesis

**Evidence balance:** strongly-bearish — a broad, sustained FY26 earnings reset (EBITDA -41%) atop elevated leverage and a largely earned peer discount.

### Bull case (overshoot / mean-revert)
- The business is self-funding on an LTM basis — LTM Levered FCF +$182.7M and FCF sign positive — supporting solvency rather than terminal distress. [7]
- The company is buying back stock (129.2M → 116.3M shares, -10.0% over ~1.7y) and interest coverage is 4.0x, consistent with a self-funding capital posture. [8]
