- The EBITDA guidance track record is 23/23 met, and the fresh FQ3 2026 adjusted-EBITDA guide of $2,860–$2,960M (filed 2026-08-05) is the current, unbeaten-cadence outlook. [25]

### Decisive question
**Is the drop from ~40x to 19.2x trailing EV/EBITDA a mean-revert overshoot on a franchise whose absolute EBITDA, FCF and segment operating income are still compounding, or an earned de-rate pricing a genuine deceleration/earnings-quality erosion the multiple has correctly begun to discount?**

- Trailing-12-month free cash flow exceeded $10B for the first time, growing non-GAAP EPS 35% YoY on operating leverage. [31]
- Absolute EBITDA rose from $6,312M (FY25) to $7,474M LTM even as the margin % stayed low, showing the de-rate is not tracking a profit decline. [10]

### …evidence against
- Consensus FY26 Net Income (Excl. Excep) has been cut ~25% over the trailing estimate path, a real earnings-quality deterioration behind the GAAP line. [12]
- G&A rose 40% in Q2 2026 on a $138M jump in legal-related accruals, a cost-line pressure that can persist below the segment-margin story. [11]
- Mobility revenue grew only 1% in Q2 2026 despite 22% Gross Bookings growth, partly on UK business-model changes — a take-rate/mix headwind the headline volume masks. [11]
