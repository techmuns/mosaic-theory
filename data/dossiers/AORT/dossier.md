### Decisive question
**Is the stent-graft/AMDS deceleration plus the On-X recall a transient stumble against an intact double-digit-growth, operating-leverage thesis, or the first evidence of a structural growth-and-cash impairment that makes the leverage and falling EPS the real story?**

### …evidence for
- Management frames Q1 AMDS softness as timing (VAC-process delays, upfront $100K stocking cost) with encouraging reorder patterns and imminent PMA approval expected to accelerate new-account conversion. [10]
- Adjusted EBITDA grew ~26% ($17.5M→$22.1M) with a 130bp margin gain in Q1 2026, evidencing that operating leverage is real even amid the revenue miss. [10]
- Absolute EBITDA has climbed from $15M (FY23) to $61M LTM, and consensus revenue held roughly flat (+3% net over 12m) even as EPS was cut — the top line is not what broke. [28]

### …evidence against
- The On-X voluntary recall lands directly on the highest-growth product line and post-dates the last earnings call, so its franchise impact is not yet in any guide. [14]
- Guidance was formally lowered and the largest category (stent grafts, 10% cc) undershot on both U.S. AMDS and international demand — a demand-side miss, not solely a cost or FX item. [25]
- FY26 GAAP EPS consensus fell ~78% and levered FCF is negative, so a leverage of 3.7x total-debt/EBITDA against 2.5x coverage cannot be de-risked from internal cash today. [28]

### Key unknowns
- Scope and financial magnitude of the May-23-2026 On-X voluntary recall (units, revenue at risk, remediation cost) — not disclosed in the supplied facts; an opaque but franchise-relevant risk.
- No forward/adjusted EV/EBITDA or adjusted EPS in the revised 99.1 guidance, and no adjusted net-debt/EBITDA — the cheap-overshoot vs cheap-terminal question cannot be settled on the trailing multiple alone.
- No segment-level EBITDA breakout (Segments sheet has no EBITDA section) — cannot see which of Products (78%) vs Preservation Services (22%) carries the profit, a bearish disclosure gap.
- Endospan acquisition terms, purchase price, financing draw, and pro-forma leverage impact are unquantified — the deal closes into an already-levered balance sheet.
- Note the internal leverage discrepancy: management cites a Q1 net-leverage ratio of 1.8x (company-adjusted basis) while CIQ shows net-debt/EBITDA 3.3x — the covenant/adjusted basis is UNKNOWN and must be reconciled.
- Whether the near-term AMDS PMA approval and NEXUS U.S. launch (targeted Jan-2027) arrive on the stated timeline — the entire re-acceleration case depends on it.
