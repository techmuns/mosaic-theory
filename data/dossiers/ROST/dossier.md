### Decisive question
**Is the recent double-digit comp surge a durable, structurally higher growth rate that justifies the premium being paid, or is it a tariff-refund- and tax-refund-aided peak that normalizes as the company laps its hardest comparisons?**

### …evidence for
- Comp growth is traffic/transaction-led across income levels and geographies for consecutive quarters, with management raising store openings to 115 — a demand-funnel mechanism, not a pricing or one-quarter spike. [10]
- Consensus has lifted FY2027 EBITDA +22% over twelve months and FY2027 normalized EPS +21%, with next-two-quarter revisions running overwhelmingly upward, corroborating a higher base rather than a fade. [11]

### …evidence against
- ~$253M of LTM EBITDA and ~$0.60 of FY2026 EPS are non-recurring IEEPA tariff refunds, and 405bps of the Q2 gross-margin gain came from those refunds — stripping them leaves operating-margin expansion of 205bps, so the headline strength overstates the run-rate. [10]
- Management conceded part of the outsized H1 comp reflected higher tax refunds and an Easter shift, and that the back half faces significantly more challenging year-over-year comparisons. [10]

### Key unknowns
- No forward adjusted-EBITDA or adjusted-EPS guide is readable at this valuation, so EV/EBITDA is trailing-only and cannot separate a temporarily tariff-boosted level from a sustainable one — an opacity that biases the headline multiple downward (flatteringly).
- The peer set is explicitly HETEROGENEOUS — market caps span $234M–$143.1B (611x) against ROST's $75.4B — so every peer-median multiple is a confidence-gated input, not a clean benchmark for the earned-vs-residual judgment.
- The amount of the H1 comp attributable to tax refunds / Easter timing vs structural customer acquisition is management-characterized but not quantified — the single biggest input to durability.
- Whether consensus EBITDA estimates are on a GAAP or adjusted basis is unresolved (implied-D&A sits in the 1.5–2.0x band where a silent basis change has occurred on this fleet) — the forward multiples may not be like-for-like with the trailing figure.

- The $241.2M 4.700% note matures 2027-04-15 and covenant terms could not be extracted (no agreement exhibit located) — minor given 0.1x leverage, but the covenant basis is UNKNOWN.
