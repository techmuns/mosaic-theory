### Decisive question
**Is the current transaction-led, double-digit comp surge a durable step-change in the reinvestment runway that justifies a premium well above the name's own history — or a cyclically inflated (tax-refund + tariff-refund) peak that normalises back toward the ~4% pre-pandemic comp, leaving the near-ceiling multiple exposed?**

### …evidence for
- Comps were transaction/traffic-driven across income levels and age groups for multiple consecutive quarters, and management frames the customer-acquisition 'flywheel' as early-stage with significant runway remaining. [10]
- The store-opening plan was raised (to 115 in 2026, 51 in Q3) and consensus FY2027 EBITDA/EPS were revised up double digits, indicating the runway is being underwritten forward, not just backward. [11]

### …evidence against
- Management attributes part of the outsized comp to higher tax refunds and idiosyncratic Q1 pent-up demand — cyclical aids that need not repeat. [27]
- 405bp of the 625bp Q2 gross-margin gain was tariff refunds and ~$0.60 of FY EPS is refund-driven, so the underlying margin improvement (~205bp) is far below the reported print — a peak the trailing 87th-percentile multiple may be extrapolating. [10]
