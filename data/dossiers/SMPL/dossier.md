### Decisive question
**Is SMPL's takeaway decline an execution problem being actively fixed (turnaround/overshoot) or a structural loss of relevance in a category that is growing without it (value trap)?**

### …evidence for
- Management frames the challenge as execution-driven not category-driven, and two brands (Quest +1.1%, OWYN +3.6%) already grew net sales ahead of plan in Q3, with a September price increase as a near-term margin lever. [11]
- Leverage and credit are peer-best (net-debt/EBITDA 1.5x, coverage 10.9x, S&P health 1/2/2/1), giving the turnaround runway to self-fund from positive FCF. [10]

### …evidence against
- Retail takeaway declined 6.7% while the category grew 10% — SMPL is ceding share, and Atkins takeaway fell 23.9% on structural household-penetration loss, the causal driver that must reverse for the overshoot thesis. [11]
- The forward guide itself embeds the decline (net sales -10% to -7%, adjusted EBITDA -22% to -19% YoY) and forward EPS breadth stays 1↑/6↓ — consensus has not yet seen a stabilization. [12]
