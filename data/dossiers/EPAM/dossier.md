### Decisive question
**Will the won/pipeline large AI-led deals plus the North America go-to-market rebuild reaccelerate organic growth in 2027 enough to re-rate the multiple — or is slow single-digit growth with a stalled largest region the new structural state (a value trap)?**

### …evidence for
- Three large multiyear deals (TCV high-tens-of-millions to over $100M[12] each) are signed within financial services and energy and are expected to ramp in H1 2027, with more expected to close — a dated, named growth catalyst beyond the current run-rate. [12]
  - How each figure is sourced: over $100M [12]
- AI-native revenue is compounding fast — over $185M[12] in Q3 (~13%[12] of revenue, over 130% YoY) with the full-year target raised to at least $650M[12] — and management frames a ~5% share as the threshold where it lifts total EPAM growth. [12]
  - How each figure is sourced: $185M [12] · ~13% [12] · $650M [12]
- The Americas grew sequentially for the second quarter in a row and net delivery headcount is being added (and expected to grow faster in Q4) to meet demand — early operating evidence that the go-to-market rebuild is moving from plan to delivery. [12]

### …evidence against
- Q4 organic cc guidance is only +0.5% to +1.6%[15] and management explicitly 'will not call the turn' in North America, expecting it to keep underperforming — the reacceleration remains unproven and deferred, with FY27 explicitly not double-digit. [15]
  - How each figure is sourced: +0.5% to +1.6% [15]
- The non-AI business declined ~6%[12] YoY while software/hi-tech (-6.1%[12]), business information & media (-5.5%[12]) and consumer goods/retail/travel (-2.5%[12]) all shrank in Q3 — the AI-native ramp is partly cannibalising/offsetting, not clearly net-additive to the whole book yet. [12]
  - How each figure is sourced: ~6% [12] · -6.1% [12] · -5.5% [12] · -2.5% [12]
- The win ratio on these large deals is unproven — management concedes it is unlikely to be materially higher than EPAM's average and that sizing/timing has repeatedly run longer than expected — so the H1-2027 ramp is a forecast, not a booked floor. [12]
